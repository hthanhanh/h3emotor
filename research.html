Research

Connecting machine design with practical engineering.

I work on the design, modelling and optimisation of electrical machines for aircraft propulsion and electric vehicles.

My research connects electromagnetic performance with winding technology, material behaviour and manufacturing considerations to develop efficient machines with high power density.

I combine finite element analysis, optimisation and experimental characterisation to understand how choices at the material and conductor levels influence the complete machine.

Machine architecture

Aircraft propulsion machines

Design and analysis of high-power-density permanent magnet machines for electrified aircraft, including MW-class machines, five-stage Halbach magnet arrays and multiphase architectures.

Focus: the balance between efficiency, active mass and winding loss.

Related work spans SPM, IPMSM, PMa-SynRM, FI-IPMSM and VFI-IPMSM topologies for transport and generation.

Related aircraft propulsion paper

Winding technology

Hairpin and continuous hairpin windings

Investigation of conductor arrangements for high-frequency operation, including flat-wound and sword-wound continuous hairpin configurations.

Focus: skin and proximity effects, circulating currents, parallel-path balance and manufacturing considerations.

Litz wire and edgewise coils provide further options for balancing AC loss, slot utilisation and manufacturability.

Related winding comparison paper

Materials & manufacturing

Electrical steels and manufacturing effects

Experimental characterisation and modelling of manufacturing-induced changes in magnetic behaviour, connecting measured B–H characteristics and iron losses with machine torque and efficiency.

Focus: cutting processes, ring-specimen measurements and material-to-machine analysis.

Additive manufacturing is a further research direction for new geometries and integrated components, guided by material properties and electromagnetic constraints.

Related electrical-steel review

Traction motors and high-speed generators

Design and optimisation of permanent magnet and permanent magnet-assisted synchronous reluctance machines for transport and generation.

Focus: torque production, field weakening, demagnetisation and high-speed electromagnetic performance.

  • 200 kW high-speed generation: micro-turbine generators.
  • 150 kW EV traction: IPMSM and axial-flux permanent magnet motor architectures.
