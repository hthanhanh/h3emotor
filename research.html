Research

Connecting machine design with practical engineering.

I work on the design, modelling and optimisation of electrical machines for electric vehicles, aircraft and UAV propulsion, high-speed generation and precision motion.

My research connects rotor and magnet architecture, winding technology, magnetic materials, electric drives and manufacturing. The aim is to understand how design choices influence torque, efficiency, power density, thermal behaviour and operation across a wide speed range.

I combine finite element analysis, analytical and magnetic-circuit models, multi-objective optimisation, surrogate modelling and experimental characterisation. The research themes below link to related journal articles and conference papers.

Electric vehicle applications

EV traction and drive-cycle performance

My EV research compares IPMSM and PM-assisted synchronous reluctance machines under current and voltage constraints. It considers torque production, flux weakening, losses and thermal behaviour under urban and highway driving cycles, connecting machine characteristics with the demands of vehicle operation.

Related studies examine traction units combining different motor types, efficient operating regions for spoke-type e-bike motors, and conductor insulation and cooling. Industrial experience includes 150 kW IPMSM and 10 kW axial-PM traction motor designs.

  • EV motor performance under driving cycles · Energies, 2018
  • Traction units combining two motor types · IEEE TVT, 2022
  • Efficient operating range of a spoke-type e-bike motor · IEEE TIA, 2022

Rotor and magnet design

Flux intensification and demagnetisation

This work investigates spoke-type IPM architectures, flux barriers, asymmetric magnet arrangements and hybrid-magnet concepts for wide-speed traction drives. It connects the rotor magnetic circuit with reluctance torque, permanent-magnet operating conditions and irreversible demagnetisation.

Operating-region analysis separates the roles of flux intensification and variable leakage flux. Related publications examine reduced-rare-earth hybrid magnets, additional rotor flux barriers and asymmetric PM configurations for EV applications.

  • Flux intensification and variable leakage flux · IEEE TIA, 2026
  • Reduced-rare-earth hybrid-magnet spoke-type motor · IEEE TMAG, 2026
  • Flux-barrier effects on spoke-type IPM motors · HUIT Journal of Science, 2026
  • Asymmetric PM configurations and EV motor demagnetisation · ICEMS, 2025
  • FI and variable-leakage-flux comparison · IAS, 2025

Aircraft and UAV applications

Aircraft and UAV propulsion machines

Aircraft propulsion work focuses on MW-class permanent-magnet machines with Halbach magnet arrays and multiphase windings. It compares Litz wire and hairpin winding solutions and studies the balance between active mass, winding loss, efficiency and power density.

Related UAV research examines six-phase outer-rotor PMSMs, including winding arrangement, phase inductance and inverter-induced harmonic effects. Design optimisation combines electromagnetic analysis with surrogate modelling and multi-objective search.

  • MW-class Litz wire and hairpin comparison · IEEE TMAG, 2025
  • Multiphase hairpin aircraft propulsion design · VPPC, 2024
  • Six-phase PMSM design considering inverter harmonics · IEEE TMAG, 2026

EV and aircraft winding systems

Hairpin and continuous hairpin windings

Winding research connects conductor geometry and winding layout with high-frequency copper losses, current sharing, thermal behaviour and manufacturability. It covers hairpin, continuous hairpin, Litz wire and edgewise coil technologies across EV traction and aircraft applications.

Publications compare different multiphase hairpin configurations and their losses and thermal behaviour. More recent EV work uses a physics-informed surrogate framework to study the local sensitivity of stator slot geometry in a 150 kW hairpin-winding IPMSM when simulation data are limited.

  • Continuous hairpin winding technology review · IEEE OJVT, 2025
  • Losses and thermal behaviour of hairpin configurations · IAS, 2025
  • Physics-informed sensitivity analysis of EV stator slots · ICEM, 2026
  • Comparison of two continuous hairpin configurations · ICEM, 2026

Materials and manufacturing

Electrical steels and manufacturing effects

My materials research connects manufacturing-induced magnetic degradation with traction motor performance. Cutting-process studies and ring-specimen measurements provide B–H characteristics and iron-loss information for material and machine-level models.

The publication record includes a review of electrical-steel cutting techniques using PMa-SynRel case studies, dynamic B–H and iron-loss modelling, and earlier investigation of thin electrical steels in IPM motors. Additive manufacturing remains a further research interest for new geometries and integrated machine components.

  • Electrical-steel cutting techniques and PMa-SynRel case studies · Results in Engineering, 2026
  • Closed-loop dynamic B–H and iron-loss modelling · INTERMAG, 2026
  • IPM motors using thin electrical steels · IEEJ JIA, 2017
  • Lamination stacking and joining review · IEEE Access, accepted

Design methods and electric drives

Physics-informed design and electric drives

I use analytical models and finite element analysis alongside design of experiments, response-surface methods, multi-objective genetic algorithms and surrogate modelling. Publications include a machine-learning design platform for PMSMs and physics-informed local sensitivity analysis for hairpin-winding EV motors.

My drive and control experience includes field-oriented control, direct torque control, field weakening and MATLAB/Simulink modelling. These methods connect electromagnetic design with current and voltage limits, inverter harmonics and operating requirements.

  • Physics-informed sensitivity analysis with limited data · ICEM, 2026
  • Machine-learning PMSM design platform · IEEE TMAG, 2023
  • Surrogate-assisted optimisation and inverter effects · IEEE TMAG, 2026

Industrial design and validation

Traction motors and high-speed generators

My industrial and academic experience spans EV traction motors and micro-turbine generation. Work at Gongin Precision Industries and National Cheng Kung University included 200 kW high-speed generators, coupled electromagnetic, structural and thermal analysis, and validation experiments.

Other project experience includes 35 kW micro-turbine generation, 10 kW and 60 kW IPM traction motors using different electrical steels, and larger traction-machine concepts using hairpin, edgewise and Litz conductors. Related published work investigates magnet configuration and post-assembly magnetisation in high-speed generators.

  • High-speed generator magnet configuration and magnetisation · IFEEC, 2019

Precision motion

Hybrid stepper motors and precision actuation

Hybrid stepper motor research examines how tooth alignment and magnetic saturation influence flux linkage, back EMF and torque. Three-dimensional finite element analysis supports refinement of tooth geometry, split ratio and multi-stack rotor arrangements, with experimental comparison against a benchmark machine.

Broader project experience includes electromagnetic design of an eight-pole permanent-magnet reaction spherical motor for satellite attitude control. Precision-motion applications connect machine architecture with torque production, packaging and operating constraints.

  • Saturation-aware electromagnetic design of hybrid stepper motors · IEEE TMAG, 2026
