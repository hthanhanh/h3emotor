Biography

Research across the UK and Taiwan.

I received my PhD in Systems and Naval Mechatronic Engineering from National Cheng Kung University in 2018. My subsequent research in Taiwan included electric vehicle traction motors and high-speed generators. At the University of Nottingham, my work has focused on aircraft propulsion machines and advanced winding technologies.

Across these areas, I am interested in connecting detailed electromagnetic analysis with material behaviour, winding design and practical manufacturing requirements.

Research collaboration

Working on the next engineering challenge.

I welcome discussions on electrical machine design, winding technologies, magnetic materials and collaborative research for electrified transport.
