Biography

Research across the UK and Taiwan.

I received my PhD in Systems and Naval Mechatronic Engineering from National Cheng Kung University in 2018. My subsequent research and industrial work in Taiwan and Vietnam included electric vehicle traction motors, high-speed generators and precision actuation. At the University of Nottingham, my work has focused on aircraft propulsion machines and advanced winding technologies.

Across these areas, I am interested in connecting detailed electromagnetic analysis with material behaviour, winding design and practical manufacturing requirements.
